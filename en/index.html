Initial estimate. The final number comes from the assessment, with your data.

How we calculate

Hours per month = people × hours per week × 4.33 weeks. Cost = hours × hourly cost (salary plus benefits and payroll taxes). The 40% to 70% reduction range is conservative for repetitive tasks such as data entry, reconciliations, record updates and reports. In the assessment, the calculation uses the real data from your process.
